There are two versions of the fee schedule with different amounts:

- **consumer-fee-schedule.html**: The overdraft fee is $29 per item paid (and returned item fee $29 per item returned), with no more than three such fees charged per account per business day. No fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at day's end [consumer-fee-schedule.html].
- **consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html**: The overdraft fee is $35 per item paid (and returned item fee $35 per item returned), with no more than four such fees charged per account per business day. This version does not mention a $10 cushion exemption [consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html].

Both versions note that an overdraft transfer from a linked savings account is free [consumer-fee-schedule.html][consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html]. Since the sources conflict (likely due to a policy update), you may want to confirm which version (v7 or the original) is currently in effect.
