## Step 2: Revenue & Fit Analysis

**Riverside Farmers Market**

**Union Station concourse**

**Campus library atrium**

- 4-week profit: $3,600 − $2,400 = **$1,200**

## Step 3: Weighing the Options

Union Station is out — it busts the budget and doesn't come close to earning that back. Between the other two, Riverside and Campus library pull in the exact same number of buyers (720 over four weeks), but Riverside's whole-bean, higher-dollar sales generate more than 5x the profit on roughly $1,200 more in rent. That's not a close call — Riverside pays for itself many times over, fits our brand (quality beans, sustainability-minded shoppers), and leaves us comfortably under the $5,000 cap.
